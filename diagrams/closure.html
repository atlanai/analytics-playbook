PopulationWhich rows are in
new-business and upsell deals past the earliest stages, on the top N accounts by current ARR
GrainOne row per what
one row per customer; each open deal counted once
Time ruleWhich period, as of when
the quarter-end snapshot at the as-of date
FormulaThe arithmetic
sum of ARR
ExclusionsWhat is always out
early stages never; internal and test accounts out; closed deals out
WITH top_accounts AS (
  SELECT account_id FROM latest_term WHERE rn = 1
  GROUP BY account_id ORDER BY SUM(arr) DESC LIMIT $n
)
SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT o.account_id) AS accounts,
       COUNT(*)                     AS deals,
       SUM(o.arr)                   AS open_pipeline
FROM crm.opportunity_snapshot o
JOIN top_accounts ta ON ta.account_id = o.account_id
JOIN crm.account a   ON a.id = o.account_id
WHERE o.as_of_date = $as_of
  AND o.stage_order >= 2
  AND o.type IN ('New Business', 'Upsell')
  AND o.is_closed = FALSE
  AND a.is_internal = FALSE
  AND a.is_test = FALSE;
