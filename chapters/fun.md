# Just for fun {#fun}

> [!RULE]
> Get it wrong here first, where it costs nothing.

Two short games built on this book's running example and its measured results. Each takes about five minutes, and both work on a phone.

## The maze {#fun-maze}

An analyst is stuck between dashboards and a queue of data tickets, and a builder wants to get them out. Pick a door: hand-picked tables, a dashboard for every question, every table with a bigger model, more documentation, or the method. Four of the five are dead ends.

[Play the maze](fun/the-maze.html)

## Guess the context {#fun-guess}

Ten kinds of context, each measured on the same real questions. Guess how many questions out of every 100 the agent gets right, and land within 10 points to score. Your total out of 10 puts you on the context ladder.

[Play Guess the Context](fun/guess-the-context.html)
