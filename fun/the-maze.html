The Maze

An analyst is drowning in data tickets. A builder wants to get them out. Meet the team, see the way out, then pick a door. Four of the five are dead ends.

  1. Meet the analyst Stuck between dashboards nobody opens and a help desk queue that never empties.

    It’s literally on the dashboard…

  2. The builder Works with the analyst. Builds the agent.

    If the agent takes the easy questions, you get your weeks back!

  3. The sponsor VP of data. Promised the board an AI win.

    Can we demo it at the next board meeting?

  4. Finance owner Owns the number the board sees.

    Close enough is still wrong.

  5. The agent Fast at SQL. New to your business.

    Ask me anything!

  6. The way out People get answers they trust in seconds. The analyst finally gets to the big project.

    Seconds, and it matches.

    No tickets today. Back to the big project!
